How to play

Slide & slide jump

Hold Ctrl (or C) while running for a speed boost that keeps your momentum and fits under low gaps. Press Space mid-slide to launch with extra speed.

Bunny hop & air strafe

Jump the moment you land and you lose no speed (hold Space for auto-hop). In the air hold A or D and turn the mouse the same way to gain speed.

Wall run & wall jump

Jump beside a teal striped wall while holding forward to run along it. Press Space on any wall to push off towards where you look.

Pads & ramps

Launch pads fling you across the arena. Run up a ramp and jump off its end to carry the climb into the air.

Weapons keys 1–4 or mouse wheel

1 Assault Rifle (full-auto) · 2 Handgun (semi-auto) · 3 Fists · 4 Grenade. F swaps to your previous weapon. Right mouse aims down sights with the rifle and handgun.

Fists & double jump

Equip the Fists and press Space once more in the air for a second jump. It refreshes when you touch the ground and works only while Fists are in your hands.

Grenade

Hold a mouse button to pull the pin and aim: a line shows the path and a circle shows where it lands. Release to throw. Keep holding: after 1 s the circle starts sliding towards you, slowly and then faster, and 3 s after the pin came out it touches you and explodes: a free grenade boost. 75 damage to enemies inside the 6 m blast (cover reduces it), never to you. One grenade, 30 s cooldown. R cancels.

Zero recoil, zero spread

Guns are hitscan: the instant you click, a perfectly straight ray goes through the crosshair. No recoil, no camera shake, no bullet drop. Dodging and positioning win fights.
